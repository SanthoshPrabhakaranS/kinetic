# Privacy Policy

**LiftLog** — Last updated: August 2026

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how LiftLog ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide through Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

- Your **name**
- Your **email address**
- Your **profile picture** (if you grant access)

We use this information only to identify you and personalize your account. We do not share it with any third party.

### 2.2 Data you create in the App

While using the App you create and store:

- Your **profile preferences** (display name, preferred weight unit: kg or lbs, selected routine type)
- Your **workout logs** (exercises, sets, repetitions, weight used, workout dates)
- Your **body weight entries** (weight and date)

### 2.3 Automatic technical data

When you use the App, standard service information may be processed by our hosting and authentication provider (Supabase), including device type, operating system, app version, and network status. This is used only to operate and secure the service.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Authenticate you and maintain your account
- Store, sync, and display your workout and weight data across your devices
- Provide app features such as progress tracking and personal records
- Send you important service communications (if applicable)

We **do not**:

- Sell your personal data
- Use your data for advertising
- Use your data for any automated decision-making

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in a cloud database (Supabase) and is protected using industry-standard security measures. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Your workout and weight data is tied to your account and is accessible only by you.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information only:

- To comply with a legal obligation or valid legal process
- To protect the rights and safety of our users and the public
- With service providers who help us operate the App (such as our hosting provider), who are bound by confidentiality obligations

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your data at any time by:

- Deleting your account in the App (if available), or
- Contacting us (see Section 9) to request deletion of your data

When you request deletion, your profile, workout logs, and weight entries are permanently removed from our servers.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or by other means.
